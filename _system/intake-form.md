# Client Intake Form

> Rebuild as a Google Form or GHL form so answers land in the CRM. Sections 1–4 are required before a build starts. Section 7 is the one clients rush — slow them down there, it's where the personality comes from.
>
> Realistic completion time: 20–30 minutes. Say so up front or they'll abandon halfway.

## 1 — Basics

1. Legal business name
2. Name for the website (how customers say it)
3. Domain — do you own it? Where is it registered?
4. Main phone number (the one that should ring)
5. Can that number receive texts?
6. Email for form submissions
7. Street address. Public, or service-area only?
8. Hours. Emergency / after-hours?
9. Year founded
10. Licence number(s) and issuing state
11. Insurance / bonding to mention
12. Google Business Profile link
13. Facebook / Instagram / TikTok / Yelp / Nextdoor
14. Do you serve Spanish-speaking customers? Need a Spanish site?

## 2 — Service area

15. Primary city
16. Every city, town, or county you'll travel to — list them all, these become pages
17. Anywhere you specifically won't go
18. A city you want more work from than you currently get?

## 3 — Services

19. Every service you offer. **Star your top three moneymakers**
20. Which would you like more of? Less of?
21. Anything you're known for that competitors don't do?
22. Pricing posture: show real prices / show ranges / no prices, estimate required
23. Free estimates? Any conditions?
24. Financing? Through whom?
25. Warranties or guarantees — the exact wording you'll stand behind
26. Brands, products, or certifications you carry

## 4 — How customers reach you

27. How do customers book today? (phone / text / form / scheduler / walk-in)
28. Which scheduler, and can you grant access?
29. Who answers the phone during hours? After hours?
30. Average response time to a web lead — honestly
31. Want an AI chat assistant that answers common questions and books appointments?
32. Collect emails for a newsletter or promotions?

## 5 — Proof

33. Paste 5–10 of your best reviews (or confirm we can pull from Google)
34. Awards, certifications, associations, press?
35. Notable jobs or clients you can name publicly?
36. Any customers willing to be photographed or quoted by name?

## 6 — Assets

37. Upload your logo. **Vector (.ai, .eps, .svg, .pdf) if you have it** — a logo pulled off Facebook will look blurry on a modern screen
38. Brand style guide or written brand colors? Upload or list hex codes
39. Upload photos. **More is better — 50+ if you have them.** Aim for:
    - Completed work · work in progress · your team with faces visible · you, the owner · trucks or vehicles · shop, showroom, or storefront · equipment and products · before-and-after pairs at matched angles
40. Photograph your vehicle wraps, uniforms, signage, and business cards. **This tells us more about your brand than a color code does**
41. Any video? Even phone footage
42. Anything we should NOT use? Old logo, former employees, a job you'd rather not showcase?

## 7 — Who you are *(the important part)*

> Answer in your own words. Voice memos welcome — record and send, we'll transcribe. Don't try to sound professional; sound like yourself.

43. **Why did you start this business?** The real reason
44. **What do you do differently** from other companies in your area? Be specific — "quality work" and "great customer service" tell us nothing
45. **Describe your ideal customer.** Age, income, homeowner vs renter, what kind of house or neighborhood, what they care about
46. **What's the moment a customer decides to call you?** What just happened in their life?
47. **What do customers say about you** that surprises you?
48. **What's the biggest objection** you have to overcome to close a job?
49. **Pick five words** you want people to feel when they land on your site
50. **Pick five words you never want** associated with your business
51. **Which is closest to your vibe?** (pick one or two, or write your own)
    - Rugged / hard-working / blue collar
    - Polished / premium / high-end
    - Warm / family / neighborly
    - Fast / urgent / dependable
    - Modern / technical / precise
    - Fun / playful / lighthearted
    - Classic / traditional / heritage
    - Bold / loud / can't-miss-us
52. **Name two or three websites you like** — any industry. What do you like about them?
53. **Name a website you hate,** or a competitor whose site you'd never want to resemble. Why?
54. **The one thing** you want every visitor to do on your site
55. **A story, phrase, or saying** that's part of how you run the business?
56. **Are you comfortable being on camera** and being the face of the brand? Both answers work — we just design differently

## 8 — Content and access

57. Want a blog? Would you write it, or should we?
58. Should individual team members have profile pages with their own booking links?
59. Main point of contact, and the fastest way to reach them
60. Any deadline — grand opening, season, ad campaign?
61. Can you grant access to: domain registrar · Google Business Profile · Google Analytics · current host · social accounts?

## Internal notes — not sent to the client

Fill in after the sales call. Usually worth more than the form.

- How does the owner actually talk? Fast, slow, formal, profane, funny?
- What did they get animated about?
- What did they complain about?
- Do they have taste, or will they ask for a rainbow gradient?
- Who really decides — owner, spouse, office manager?
- Expected photo grade: A / B / C
- Red flags?
