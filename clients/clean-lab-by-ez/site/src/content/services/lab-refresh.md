## The maintenance detail

Lab Refresh is the tier for a car that's already in decent shape and just needs to stay that way. It's not a deep clean — it's the wash-and-tidy you book every few weeks so a deep clean never has to happen in the first place. We show up with everything we need — water, power, and the gear — and do the whole thing in your driveway or parking spot while you go about your day.

## What's included

- Foam cannon pre-wash, followed by a full exterior hand wash
- Wheel and tire cleaning, finished with tire shine
- Interior vacuum
- Dashboard, console, and panel wipe-down
- Interior glass

That's exterior and interior covered in one visit — the wash gets the grime off the paint, and the vacuum-and-wipe-down handles the everyday interior stuff: crumbs, dust, fingerprints on the console, smudged windows.

## Why this matters more in Central Florida

Love bugs are a real seasonal headache for anyone parking a car outside in Central Florida — the swarms hit hardest in late spring and again in early fall, and their residue etches into clear coat and grille plastic the longer it sits. A foam cannon pre-wash is exactly the right tool for that: it loosens bug residue and light film before a cloth ever touches the paint, instead of dragging it across the surface. If you're parking outside through love bug season, a Lab Refresh every few weeks does more than keep the car looking good — it protects the finish.

## Who books this

Lab Refresh is built for routine upkeep, not a rescue job. It's the right call if your car looks fine but could use a wash, if you're keeping up a regular schedule so dirt and grime never get the chance to set in, or if you just want the inside vacuumed and wiped down without a full interior overhaul. If your car has stains, odor, or hasn't been cleaned in a while, Lab Reset is the better starting point — Lab Refresh is for staying ahead of that, not catching up to it.
