Lab Reset is Clean Lab by EZ's most-booked package — it's listed as "Most Popular" on our own price list, and it's the tier for a car that needs more than a wash. - Deep interior clean

## When stains need extra work

Stain extraction is included in every Lab Reset, but some stains — a spill that sat for weeks, a bigger mess — need more time and product than the base service covers. Extra stain extraction runs $25-40 on top of the Lab Reset price, quoted once we see what we're working with.

## Why Central Florida cars need this more often

Humidity is the quiet enemy of a car's interior here. A spill or a wet mat that would air out fast in a drier climate instead sits and works into the carpet padding, and that's exactly what turns into the odor problem Lab Reset is built to solve. Between afternoon rain for a good chunk of the year, beach and lake trips, and cars that spend more time parked outside than in a garage, Central Florida interiors pick up moisture, mildew, and odor faster than most. If it's been a while since a deep interior clean, that's usually why.
