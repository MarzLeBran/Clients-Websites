## Pet hair removal

Pet hair removal is priced at $25 — it's listed on our price list both as its own add-on service and as an option to pair with either Lab Refresh or Lab Reset. If you're regularly hauling a dog or cat around and the hair has worked its way into the seat fabric and carpet, this is the line item to add when you book.


## How to book it

Because pet hair removal is most useful paired with a full detail, most customers add it on top of a Lab Refresh or Lab Reset rather than booking it alone. Mention it when you call or text (689) 200-6472, or note it in the contact form, so we can plan for it.

## Mobile, like everything else

Same as every Clean Lab by EZ service, pet hair removal is done at your home or workplace — we bring our own water and electricity, so there's nothing you need to set up ahead of time beyond having the vehicle accessible.
