## Engine bay cleaning

Engine bay cleaning is one of our additional, custom services — alongside pet hair removal, it's listed on our price list as available "per vehicle needs" rather than at a fixed price. That means we don't quote a number here; we price it once we know the vehicle.


## How to book it

If you want your engine bay cleaned, mention it when you call or text (689) 200-6472, or note it in the contact form along with your vehicle type and city. We'll quote it based on what the vehicle needs, and it can be scheduled alongside a Lab Refresh or Lab Reset appointment.

## Mobile, like everything else

As with every service, this is done wherever your vehicle is — we bring our own water and electricity to your home or workplace.
