## Haines City, FL

Haines City sits on the more rural edge of our service area — Polk County citrus country, where groves and open agricultural land border the residential streets rather than the dense subdivisions you'd find closer to Orlando or Kissimmee. The approach roads into a lot of Haines City neighborhoods are still rural in character, and that changes what a car parked outside actually collects.

Grove country produces its own kind of exterior grime — agricultural dust kicked up from grove work and unpaved or lightly-paved approach roads, which is a different problem than the urban road film you'd get from daily city driving. It settles into grille plastic, wheel wells, and paint in a way that needs a real pre-wash to lift rather than just a quick rinse.

That's where the foam cannon pre-wash in Lab Refresh earns its keep in Haines City specifically — it loosens agricultural dust and grime before any cloth touches the paint, which matters more here than in a city where cars mostly pick up ordinary road dirt. Lab Reset is the right call once that dust has worked its way past the exterior and into carpets and mats over time.
