## Kissimmee, FL

Kissimmee sits right along the theme-park corridor, and that shapes the kind of driving its vehicles take. Between the vacation-rental homes clustered near US-192 and the daily churn of visitors renting cars for a week at a time, Kissimmee sees more short-term, high-mileage vehicle turnover than a typical residential market — a rental car or a homeowner's second vehicle gets driven hard for a stretch and then needs to be turned around fast for the next guest or the next trip.

Kissimmee is also built around water — Lake Tohopekaliga anchors the western side of the city, and the humidity that comes with living near it doesn't stay outside the car. Windows left cracked, a wet towel left on a seat after a pool day, a rainy afternoon commute — in a lake-adjacent city like this, that moisture works into carpet and upholstery and turns into odor and mildew faster than it would somewhere drier.

That's why both tiers matter here for different reasons. Lab Refresh is the fast turnaround for a rental or a vehicle that just needs to look sharp again between guests — foam cannon wash, vacuum, wipe-down, done quickly. Lab Reset is the answer once humidity has actually gotten into the interior — the shampoo, stain extraction, and odor elimination work is what gets a musty interior back to normal.
