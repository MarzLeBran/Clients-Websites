## St. Cloud, FL

St. Cloud sits just east of Kissimmee, and a lot of its growth has happened along the lake-adjacent side of the city — newer residential development that's filled in over recent years, much of it closer to East Lake Tohopekaliga than to the older downtown core. That newer-build pattern means fewer mature trees and lawns in a lot of these neighborhoods, and more exposed, sandy Central Florida soil.

That sandy soil is the real local issue for a car parked outside in St. Cloud. It tracks in on shoes, blows across yards and driveways that haven't fully grassed in yet, and ends up ground into floor mats and carpet fiber in a way that's genuinely harder to vacuum out than ordinary dirt — it's fine, it's gritty, and it settles into the weave instead of sitting on top of it.

Lab Refresh handles the routine version of that — a quick interior vacuum and wipe-down keeps sand from building up between visits. But once sand has worked its way into the mats and carpet over weeks of normal use, that's a Lab Reset job: the shampoo and stain-extraction step actually pulls it out of the fiber instead of just moving it around.
