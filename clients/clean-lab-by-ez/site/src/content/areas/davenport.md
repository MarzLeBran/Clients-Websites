## Davenport, FL

Davenport is where Clean Lab by EZ is based, and it's still the market we know best. It's grown fast — the stretch along US-27 near Posner Park and the ChampionsGate corridor has filled in with gated and HOA communities over the last several years, many of them built around golf courses and short-term rental properties rather than the older grid-street neighborhoods you'd find closer to the coasts.

That growth cuts both ways for a car parked outside. Gated communities mean a lot of Davenport driveways are genuinely easy to work in — no street-parking restrictions to navigate, just pull in and get to work, which is a big part of why mobile detailing makes sense here in the first place. But the same construction that's filling in those communities means dust: cleared lots, unfinished roads, and ongoing build-out track fine grit onto paint and into floor mats in a way a finished, mature neighborhood doesn't produce as much of.

Between the two tiers, that split shows up directly. A Lab Refresh — foam cannon wash, wheel and tire clean, interior vacuum — is the regular-upkeep answer for a car parked in one of Davenport's newer gated communities. A Lab Reset earns its keep once new-construction dust has worked its way into carpets and mats over a few weeks and a vacuum alone isn't cutting it anymore.
