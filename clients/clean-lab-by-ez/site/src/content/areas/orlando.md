## Orlando, FL

Orlando is the biggest, densest market we cover, and that density is exactly why mobile detailing makes sense here. Downtown and the areas around it are full of condo towers and apartment buildings with limited, assigned, or gated parking — the kind of setup where driving to a detail shop and finding a place to leave your car for a few hours isn't simple, if it's even possible. We come to the garage or the assigned spot instead, which solves that access problem directly.

Orlando is also a commuter city — I-4 and the surface streets that feed it carry a lot of daily driving, and that shows up as a specific kind of grime: road film, brake dust, and the general buildup that comes from more pavement time than a car that mostly sits in a driveway would ever see. It's a different problem than a car that's been neglected — it's a car that's just been used, a lot, on paved urban roads.

For that kind of daily-driver grime, Lab Refresh — the foam cannon wash plus wheel and tire cleaning — is the right regular booking to keep road film and brake dust from building up. Lab Reset comes in when the interior needs the deeper work too, especially for a car that's spent a lot of time as a daily commuter rather than a weekend vehicle.
