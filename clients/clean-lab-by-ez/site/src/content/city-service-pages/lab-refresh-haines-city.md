## Lab Refresh in Haines City, FL

Haines City sits at the edge of Polk County's citrus country, and the approach roads into a lot of its neighborhoods are still rural — groves and open agricultural land bordering the residential streets, rather than the dense subdivisions closer to Orlando or Kissimmee. That produces a different kind of exterior grime than a city street would: agricultural dust from grove work and unpaved or lightly-paved roads, settling into grille plastic, wheel wells, and paint.

Lab Refresh is built to lift that before it becomes a bigger problem. The foam cannon pre-wash loosens agricultural dust and grime ahead of the exterior hand wash, instead of dragging it across the paint with a cloth, the wheels and tires get cleaned and finished with tire shine, and the interior gets vacuumed and wiped down — dashboard, console, panels, glass.

For a car regularly driving Haines City's rural approach roads, a Lab Refresh every few weeks keeps grove dust from building up and working into the finish.
