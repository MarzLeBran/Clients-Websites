## Lab Reset in Poinciana, FL

Poinciana is still filling in — one of Central Florida's largest planned communities, built out across the Polk-Osceola county line in sections rather than all at once. Wherever that build-out is still active, fine construction dust ends up tracked into cars parked nearby, and over a few weeks of normal use it works past the surface and into the carpet fiber and floor mat backing.

That's the point where a vacuum stops being enough, and Lab Reset takes over. Seat, carpet, and mat shampoo with stain extraction pulls embedded dust out of the fiber, the deep interior clean covers every surface it's settled on, and odor elimination handles anything that's built up from dust sitting in a damp mat or carpet pad. The full detail covers the exterior too, so the car comes back clean everywhere, not just refreshed on top.

If your Poinciana car's carpet or mats look dingy no matter how often you vacuum, that's usually construction dust that's worked in deeper than routine cleaning reaches.
