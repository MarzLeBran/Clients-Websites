## Lab Reset in Haines City, FL

Haines City's citrus-grove country produces a kind of dust that's genuinely different from what a car picks up in Orlando or Kissimmee — agricultural dust from grove work and rural approach roads, tracked in on shoes and worked into carpet and floor mats over repeated trips. It's finer and settles deeper than ordinary road dirt, and it doesn't come out with a quick vacuum once it's had time to work in.

Lab Reset is the tier that actually gets it out. Seat, carpet, and mat shampoo with stain extraction pulls embedded grove dust out of the fiber, the deep interior clean covers every surface it's reached, and odor elimination handles any mustiness from dust sitting in a mat or carpet pad over time. The exterior gets the same full treatment, so a car that regularly drives Haines City's rural roads comes back clean top to bottom.

If your Haines City car's interior has been picking up grove dust for a while and a vacuum isn't keeping up anymore, that's exactly what Lab Reset is built to handle.
