## Lab Refresh in Orlando, FL

If you live in a condo tower or high-rise near downtown Orlando, you already know the problem with driving to a detail shop: finding somewhere to leave the car once you get there. Assigned and gated parking solves the theft and access questions, but it doesn't solve the "now what" of getting your car cleaned. Mobile detailing solves it directly — we come to your garage or assigned spot instead.

Orlando is also a commuter city. I-4 and the surface streets that feed it carry a lot of daily driving, and that shows up as a specific kind of buildup: road film, brake dust, and general grime from pavement time rather than neglect. Lab Refresh is built for exactly that — a foam cannon pre-wash and full exterior hand wash cut through road film, the wheels and tires get cleaned and finished with tire shine, and the interior gets a vacuum along with a dashboard, console, and panel wipe-down and interior glass.

For a daily-driver Orlando commute, booking Lab Refresh regularly keeps road grime and brake dust from building up between cleans.
