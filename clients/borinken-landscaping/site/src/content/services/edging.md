Edging is what makes a yard look sharp instead of just cut. It's the clean line between grass and mulch bed, the crisp break along a paver driveway, the difference between a lawn that looks maintained and one that looks mowed-around.

It's also where the extra care shows. On one job, that meant getting up on a pole trimmer to shape an overgrown topiary into a clean, rounded ball right next to the flagpole — the kind of detail most crews would leave alone because nobody's paying specifically for it. Hiram does it anyway, because a job's not really finished until the edges and details are right.

If you've got beds, walkways, or a driveway line that's gone soft and grown-over, that's an easy fix.
