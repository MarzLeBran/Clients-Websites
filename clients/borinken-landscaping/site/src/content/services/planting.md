Planting is more than digging a hole and dropping something in. Hiram assesses the spot — sun, drainage, what's already growing nearby — before anything goes in the ground, so it has a real shot at taking root instead of struggling through its first Florida summer.

One customer's own words say it best: Hiram fixed a garden that wasn't working, planted a run of shrubs and a tree, and then took the time to walk her through how to actually care for what was just planted — watering, what to watch for — so the work didn't stop the moment he drove off.

That's the standard: shrubs, trees, and plant beds installed with some care behind them, and a homeowner who knows what to do next, not just a truck that shows up, plants, and leaves.
