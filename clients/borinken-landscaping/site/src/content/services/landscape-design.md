This is the work Hiram takes the most pride in — building a bed or a whole section of yard from the ground up, not just maintaining what's already there. A curved mulch island with a palm tree at the center and crotons planted in around it, a new bed running along a walkway, a corner of the yard that's never had anything done with it — this is where that gets planned out and installed.

It's a two-part job: figuring out what actually fits the space and the light, and then getting it in the ground clean, with lines that are cut sharp and plants set where they'll actually thrive. Hiram and whoever's helping him that day do the digging and planting together, on-site, not off a template.

If you've got a spot that's never quite come together, or you want to start something new instead of just maintaining the same yard, tell Hiram what you're picturing and he'll help you plan it out.
