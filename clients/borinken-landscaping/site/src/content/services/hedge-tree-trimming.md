This is the work that takes the most eye — a hedge that's shaped, not just hacked at, and a tree that's trimmed to let light through without leaving it looking bare. Hiram gets up on the ladder for it, whether that's shaping a big rounded hedge along a pool or lakefront, or working a pole saw up into a tree canopy that's grown out over the curb or the driveway.

An overgrown hedge or a tree branch hanging low over the walkway isn't just untidy — it's usually the first thing an HOA letter mentions. Getting it trimmed back on a regular basis keeps it from turning into a bigger job later, and keeps the whole property looking like someone's paying attention to it.

If a hedge has gotten away from you or a tree needs shaping back into line, get in touch and Hiram will take a look.
