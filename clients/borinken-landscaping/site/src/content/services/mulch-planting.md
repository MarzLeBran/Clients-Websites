A fresh bed of mulch does more to change how a yard looks than almost anything else — it's the difference between a yard that looks maintained and one that just looks mowed. Hiram lays mulch clean and even along bed lines, and installs the plants to go with it: new palm trees, foundation shrubs, whatever fits the spot and what you're going for.

This is finish work, not just labor — a bed edge that's cut sharp and a plant set at the right depth is what makes the whole thing look like it was done by someone who does this for a living, not a weekend project. If you've got bare dirt along the foundation, a bed that's gone to weeds, or you just want the front of the house to look put-together again, this is the service for that.

Tell Hiram what you're picturing and he'll walk you through what'll work for the spot.
