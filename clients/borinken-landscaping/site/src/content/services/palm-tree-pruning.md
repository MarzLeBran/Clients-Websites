Palms don't shed dead fronds on a schedule that's convenient for anyone — they just hang there, brown and heavy, until someone takes care of it. Hiram gets up a ladder with a chainsaw and takes them down clean, working right up into the crown of the tree, even on the tallest palms on the property.

Neatly pruned palms are one of those things that change the whole feel of an entrance or a front yard — two palms framing a driveway or a doorway look completely different cleaned up than they do with a head full of dead fronds. It's not a small job to do safely at that height, which is exactly why it's worth having someone who does it regularly.

If your palms are due, call or text Hiram and he'll get them cleaned up.
